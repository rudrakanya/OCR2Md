# Outline: C18 — Present Tense: How the Town Lives with Its Past Today

**Scope note:** Owns the town's present-day lived experience (anchored on Jagta Hua Kasba) -- local, oral, contemporary register. Receives from C17; hands off to AW for forward-looking challenges.

**Thesis:** Udaypur today is a town caught between quiet neglect and sudden awakening: its ancient stones survive amid encroachment and indifference, but a handful of individuals -- using social media, heritage walks, and institutional partners like INTACH -- have begun forcing the town's past back into its present, even as the deeper work of reckoning with what comes next remains undone.

## A Living Town Built Over a Dying Past
- Udaipur today is a settlement of roughly eight thousand people whose houses, shops, and cattle-sheds are built directly into and atop the ruins of a Paramara-era town, so that the ancient town's footprint is larger than the modern population's. (Udaypur: Jagta Hua Kasba (Udaypur, A Town Awake); Udaypur: Jagta Hua Kasba (Udaypur, A Town Awake))
- Dr. Suresh Mishra warned that not just the temple but the whole town needs care, since buildings are collapsing in front of people's eyes -- no foreign invader is doing this now, only present-day neglect. **[THIN — single source]** (Udaypur: Jagta Hua Kasba (Udaypur, A Town Awake))
- Ordinary residents live amid the wreckage without registering its significance: a Jain family's grocery shop opens into a thousand-year-old carved sanctum, and only three Jain families remain in the town at all. **[THIN — single source]** (Udaypur: Jagta Hua Kasba (Udaypur, A Town Awake))

## Indifference on the Ground
- Local people are described as having lost any living connection to their own heritage, with panchayats, elected representatives, and schoolteachers alike failing for decades to propose even paper action to protect Udaipur's ruins. (Udaypur: Jagta Hua Kasba (Udaypur, A Town Awake); Udaypur: Jagta Hua Kasba (Udaypur, A Town Awake))
- Most residents' attention is consumed by livelihood and farming, and even correspondence sent in the past produced no local effect. **[THIN — single source]** (Udaypur: Jagta Hua Kasba (Udaypur, A Town Awake))
- Illegal stone quarrying, encroachment, and idol theft continue today, driven by an alliance of quarry owners, local leaders, and officials who profit while 'Udaipurs are not made.' **[THIN — single source]** (Udaypur: Jagta Hua Kasba (Udaypur, A Town Awake))

## The Spark: Social Media as a Modern Heritage Weapon
- A campaign began in January 2021 when photographs of a walled palace bearing a 'Private Property' sign were tweeted to the Prime Minister, the Tourism Minister, and the Chief Minister, and posted to Facebook under the headline 'Udaipur Palace, Khasra No. 822, Private Property.' (Udaypur: Jagta Hua Kasba (Udaypur, A Town Awake); Udaypur: Jagta Hua Kasba (Udaypur, A Town Awake))
- The post reached over 12,000 readers on Facebook alone, was circulated by Dr. Mishra among historian networks, and was picked up by WhatsApp contacts among editors and reporters from Delhi to Vidisha. **[THIN — single source]** (Udaypur: Jagta Hua Kasba (Udaypur, A Town Awake))
- The social-media stir crossed into print media, with Swadesh Indore, Swadesh Bhopal, and the Patrika correspondent in Vidisha, Govind Saxena, all running the story with photographs after seeing the online posts. **[THIN — single source]** (Udaypur: Jagta Hua Kasba (Udaypur, A Town Awake))

## Institutions and Individuals Respond
- INTACH, through its Bhopal convener Dr. Madanmohan Upadhyay, a retired IAS officer, deployed an expert team that combed every lane of Udaipur, identifying remnants across a thousand to twelve hundred years and cataloguing dozens of monuments and a hundred inscriptions, with a research publication forthcoming. (Udaypur: Jagta Hua Kasba (Udaypur, A Town Awake); Udaypur: Jagta Hua Kasba (Udaypur, A Town Awake))
- Upadhyay argues that state-sponsored panchayat-level documentation, done with local volunteers in about a week per site, could surface many more unknown heritage sites the way Udaipur was surfaced. **[THIN — single source]** (Udaypur: Jagta Hua Kasba (Udaypur, A Town Awake))
- A local resident, the 57-year-old Raja Mishra, voluntarily donated eight feet of his own land to widen the temple road, prompting some forty businessmen to set back their shops -- a rare instance of local initiative outrunning government action. **[THIN — single source]** (Udaypur: Jagta Hua Kasba (Udaypur, A Town Awake))
- Journalist Vijay Manohar Tiwari and Dr. Suresh Mishra organized a recurring 'heritage walk,' bringing a rotating band of curious visitors to Udaipur every month to build awareness, a project continuing even after Mishra's death during the corona period. (Udaypur: Jagta Hua Kasba (Udaypur, A Town Awake); Udaypur: Jagta Hua Kasba (Udaypur, A Town Awake))

## Walking the Present-Day Lanes
- A first-person heritage-walk narrative documents the town today lane by lane: the Neelkantheshwar Mahadev temple, the residential compound of Bija Mandal with cattle tied inside an old temple-like structure, and a Jain temple precinct where mutilated Tirthankara images still stand. **[THIN — single source]** (Udaypur: Jagta Hua Kasba (Udaypur, A Town Awake))
- Contemporary details -- bidi-smoking and gutkha-spitting shopkeepers, an ASI caretaker summoned only for VIP darshan, tehsil officials alert for dignitaries rather than for Udaipur itself -- capture the town's present-day bureaucratic and social texture around its ruins. **[THIN — single source]** (Udaypur: Jagta Hua Kasba (Udaypur, A Town Awake))
- Even using Google Maps, walkers found stone structures of a Ram temple and old market buildings buried in neglected greenery, prompting a call for a local 'Bharat Swachhata Mission' aimed not at garbage but at centuries of dust. **[THIN — single source]** (Udaypur: Jagta Hua Kasba (Udaypur, A Town Awake))

## A Public Reckoning in Bhopal
- The campaign culminated in a public event, 'Katha Udaipur,' held at the Tribal Museum in Bhopal, drawing over a hundred attendees including senior editors, historians, and officials, despite a technically weak presentation setup. (Udaypur: Jagta Hua Kasba (Udaypur, A Town Awake); Udaypur: Jagta Hua Kasba (Udaypur, A Town Awake))
- The chapter frames Udaipur as a moral claim on 'every citizen of this region,' not just its panchayat residents, insisting that the town's meaning as a symbol of India's cultural identity obligates present-day action, not merely government intervention. (Udaypur: Jagta Hua Kasba (Udaypur, A Town Awake); Udaypur: Jagta Hua Kasba (Udaypur, A Town Awake))

## An Unresolved Present
- A polemical passage frames Udaipur's neglect within a communal narrative about medieval iconoclasm versus modern indifference, contrasting the ruined Paramara palaces with flourishing mosques and mocking contemporary commentators -- a charged present-day interpretation the town's activists themselves voice. **[THIN — single source]** (Udaypur: Jagta Hua Kasba (Udaypur, A Town Awake))
