Talking about this particular hill he writes- "To the south of the city, at a distance of about half a mile, is a hill of a very remarkable appearance; a huge mass of rock rises sheer and abrupt from the top of the conical hill below, presenting at a distance the exact appearance of a fortified castle. This, however, is not a castle, but a natural solid mass of rock, though it is evident that it could, and probably was, made use of as a fort.
At the foot, or rather some little way above the foot of the cone, however, there runs a wall built of huge uncemented stones, 18 feet thick at the present crest, which, however, is not the original crest of the wall, that having disappeared long ago; several gates existed in this wall, which did not go quite round the hill, but enclosed only a portion, in the form of a circular sector, the two radial lines of the sector being represented by two similar massive walls running straight the side of the hill up the to meet the tower-like mass above. C07-GEOHER-002   p. 9   also Ch 11
At about 100 meters from this Natraj sculpture, we see a long wall that measures 1400 meters. It is a very thick wall encircling the hillock from all side, a close examination of this 1400 m wall show that this is a fortification wall, meaning that the encircled land is part of a fort complex, which existed prior to Udayaditya, or could have been constructed during the time of Udayaditya as a military Cantonment, because this was the most secured hillock, with very steep slopes.
Walking along this hill fortification wall, we see bastions of different dimensions, the semicircular bastion, the rectangular bastion, and gateways on both sides of the entry gate.
This entire fort complex is a mystery because historical records are silent about it. It is also an architectural marvel that is standing undisturbed over a span of 1400 meters, maybe the longest one anywhere in Madhya Pradesh.

History shows that in those days there used to be a constant war between the Parmar, the Chalukyas, and the Kalachuris, and such massive fortification complexes in one part of the hill, on the upper reaches of the village gave a strategically important advantage. The vantage point of high hills to attack any approaching enemy from a distance. The open landscape all around was also an advantage. The army was staying inside this fortification.
The thickness of the fort's wall, almost 5 m thick, was not easy to break. Interestingly, in the last 1000 years it has not been breached even at a single place.

C07-GEOHER-009   p. 81
The wall is made of sandstone. The stones used are arranged in dry stone rubble masonry, the walls protect the hills from three sides, North, East, and West, while the south side of the hill is too steep to climb, hence, it does not contain any forms of built structures. The wall comprises of two types of stone sizes first the well-shaped and more defined stone used in the outer face of the wall to make it presentable where on the other hand inner void of the thick wall are filled with comparatively smaller size stones which gets easily fixed in the gaps and hold the entire wall tightly.
The Hill Fort in Udaypur has a massive boundary wall made of sandstone running along the periphery of the hill with a total length of about 1400 m. This fortification wall is made from locally available red sandstone, which is found in abundance in Udaypur.

The present condition of the fortification wall and other built structures is now completely in ruins, suggesting a strong probability that the hill must have been not merely a settlement but also had served a defensive purpose, emphasizing its strategic significance in the region.

C07-GEOHER-012   p. 88
The shapes of the bastions found on the Udaypur hill fortification wall are semi-circular, rectangular, and circular in design.
They consist of three stories, which have solid blocks of sandstone firmly placed together or bound with iron clamps on the topmost layer, which is the coping. The weight of the blocks is in tons. There are numerous peepholes on the bastion which were used for defense purposes, either to shoot arrows and guns from higher altitudes, providing an advantage to the soldiers.

What sets them apart are the interesting courtyards that bring life to the stone-cladded structures. These courtyards serve as communal spaces where residents gather, fostering a sense of community and warmth.

## कथा उदयपुर (Jagta Hua Kasba) — Hindi original (JAGTA-HI, 63)
जैसे ही हम एक बुर्ज से बाईं तरफ मुड़े, हमें सीध में दूसरा बुर्ज नजर आया। इस बुर्ज के सामने उदयपुर की मौजूदा ठसाठस बस्ती शुरू होती है। लेकिन इन दो बुर्जों के बीच हमें कुछ ऐसा दिखाई दिया, जो बिल्कुल ही अलग और ध्यान खींचने वाला था। दो बुर्जों के बीच की पत्थर की पुरानी करीब बीस फुट ऊंची राजमहल की सुरक्षा दीवार बीच से टूटी हुई थी। सीमेंट का एक लंबा और ऊंचा फ्रेम बनाया गया था। इस पर लोहे का बड़ा सा फाटक लगा था। दीवार पर एक साइन बोर्ड इसके निजी संपत्ति होने की घोषणा कर रहा था, जिस पर खसरा और वार्ड नंबर के साथ कुछ फोन नंबर भी लिखे थे।
*Gloss [my translation]:* As soon as we turned left from one bastion, we saw another bastion straight ahead. In front of this bastion the present crowded settlement of Udaypur begins. But between these two bastions we saw something quite different and attention-grabbing. The old stone defence wall of the palace, about twenty feet high, between the two bastions was broken in the middle. A long, tall cement frame had been built, with a big iron gate on it. A signboard on the wall declared it private property, with khasra and ward numbers and some phone numbers.
C07-JAGTA-HI-001   p. 14   also Ch 11
वर्ना ऐसे मंदिर गांव-गांव में हैं, लेकिन चौकोर दुर्ग से सुरक्षित नगर के भीतर एक विशाल चारदीवारी और बुर्जों से घिरे राजमहल की रूपरेखा इस इलाके में सिर्फ वहीं हैं। यह अवश्य ही उदयादित्य के पहले से बसी एक बस्ती रही होगी, जहां परमारों की सैन्य टुकड़ी सीमाओं की सुरक्षा के लिए तैनात होगी। उदयादित्य की नियुक्ति ने इस बस्ती का भाग्य जगा दिया था, जहां अब कुछ ऐसा होने वाला था, जो सदियों बाद भी स्मृतियों में जीवित रहेगा।
*Gloss [my translation]:* Otherwise such temples are in village after village, but a palace surrounded by a vast enclosure wall and bastions inside a town protected by a square fort has its outline only here in this region. This must certainly have been a settlement established before Udayāditya, where a Paramāra military contingent would be posted to guard the borders. Udayāditya's appointment awakened the fortunes of this settlement.
C07-JAGTA-HI-002   p. 18   also Ch 4
क्या मजबूत पत्थरों से बनी ऊंची चारदीवारी और बुर्जों से घिरे किसी एक हजार साल पुराने राजमहल का कोई हिस्सा किसी की निजी संपत्ति हो सकता है, जिसकी एक दीवार को तोड़कर सीमेंट और लोहे का दरवाजा लगा दिया गया हो और कोई इस तरफ ऐसी-वैसी निगाह न देखे इसके लिए पुरानी दीवार पर एक साइन बोर्ड टांग दिया गया हो- 'निजी संपत्ति, उदयपुर पैलेस, खसरा नंबर-822, वार्ड नंबर-141'
*Gloss [my translation]:* Can any part of a thousand-year-old palace surrounded by a high enclosure wall of strong stones and bastions be someone's private property, one of whose walls has been broken to fit a cement and iron door, with a signboard hung on the old wall: 'Private property, Udaypur Palace, Khasra No. 822, Ward No. 141'.
C07-JAGTA-HI-003   p. 18
परमारों के समय उदयपुर की पहाड़ी पर भी किले की सुरक्षा दीवार बनाई गई थी, जो आज भी सामने और गूगल मैप पर दिखाई देती है।
*Gloss [my translation]:* In the time of the Paramāras a fort defence wall was also built on the hill of Udaypur, which is visible today in front of us and on Google Maps.
C07-JAGTA-HI-004   p. 23
चारों ओर दुर्ग की प्राचीरों से घिरे इस नगर की शोभा में चार चांद लगने वाले थे। दुर्ग की पश्चिम की दीवार के नीचे एक विशाल जलाशय बन रहा था। वह यहां से गुजरने वाले सैन्य और व्यवसायी कार्फलों के साथ कृषकों और श्रद्धालुओं के लिए जल की आपूर्ति करेगा।
*Gloss [my translation]:* The beauty of this town, surrounded by the fort's ramparts, was to be enhanced. Below the fort's west wall a vast reservoir was being built, supplying water to passing military and merchant caravans, farmers and devotees.
C07-JAGTA-HI-005   p. 28   also Ch 2
पहाड़ी रास्तों में किले की प्राचीरों के अवशेष आपसे गुस्सा नजर आएंगे, क्योंकि उन्हें देखने कोई नहीं आता। उनके पास सदियों की गवाही है।
*Gloss [my translation]:* On the hill paths, remains of the fort ramparts look angry because no one comes to see them. They hold centuries of testimony.
C07-JAGTA-HI-006   p. 39
जब आप यह सब देखते हुए गलियों में घूमेंगे तो कई जगह पुराने पत्थर के विशाल दरवाजों से होकर गुजरेंगे। परमारों के व्यवस्थित उदयपुर में यह दरवाजे रास्तों पर लगाए गए थे। इनके आसपास कतार से वो दुकानें नजर आएंगी, जहां सदियों का मलबा भरा हुआ है। ये रौनकदार बाजार का हिस्सा रहे होंगे।
*Gloss [my translation]:* Walking the lanes you pass through large old stone gateways. In the orderly Paramara Udaipur these gates were set across roads. Beside them are rows of shops filled with centuries of debris; they must have been part of a lively market.
C07-JAGTA-HI-007   p. 39   also Ch 8
मुगलों के हाथों हुए उस बड़े हत्याकांड की बुरी खबरें उदयपुर में चंदेरी दरवाजे से ही आई होंगी, जहां मैं कुछ बाद में गया। वह घेरा मेनरोड के समानांतर रही उदयपुर के किले की दीवार का एक दरवाजा है। वह उदयपुर में उत्तरदिशा की तरफ है। उस जमाने में जिस बड़े शहर की तरफ किसी शहर का कोई दरवाजा होता, वह उसी शहर के नाम होता।
*Gloss [my translation]:* Bad news of that great massacre must have come to Udaipur through the Chanderi gate. It is a gate of Udaipur's fort wall running parallel to the main road, on the north side. In that era a city's gate was named for the big city it faced.
C07-JAGTA-HI-008   p. 49   also Ch 10
आपातकाल में सुरक्षा के उद्देश्य से बनाई गई पहाड़ी किले की प्राचीर के पीछे हर तरफ से आकर छिपे हिंदू अपने घर-बार छोड़कर यहां कुछ देर की राहत पा गए थे। नीचे मंदिर के प्रांगण से उठ रहा भारी शोर वे यहां से देख रहे होंगे।
*Gloss [my translation]:* Hindus who had come from all sides and hidden behind the hill fort's rampart, built for safety in emergencies, got brief relief there. They must have been watching from here the great noise rising from the temple courtyard below.
C07-JAGTA-HI-009   p. 106
हम उस चंदरी गेट पर भी गए जिसके बारे में पुरातत्व विभाग के ही एक अधिकारी ने बताया था कि कभी वहां एक पुराना मंदिर रहा होगा। मूर्तियों और स्तंभों के टुकड़े अब तक वहां लावारिस पड़े हैं। पांच लोग मुश्किल से नमाज पढ़ सकें इतना छोटा सा एक मस्जिदनुमा ढांचा वहीं गेट के बाहर बाद के दौर का है, जिसमें मंदिर का मलबा ही इस्तेमाल किया गया है।
*Gloss [my translation]:* We went to the Chanderi gate, about which an archaeology department officer had said an old temple must once have stood there. Pieces of images and pillars still lie orphaned.
