# Afterword. Conserving Udaypur: Community, Heritage and the Future

**Target:** 2,750 words (U3) · **Sections:** 6 · **Evidence:** `evidence_index/ch13.jsonl` (58 direct, 9 contextual), with recalls from Chs 11–12 · **Rules:** R5–R9, U7.

## 1. Function

- **Question:** What does the record add up to for the town's future: conservation of what, for whom, and at whose cost?
- **Argument:** The Afterword is built, not summarised.
  - It fixes the evidence horizon (early 2021, and a survey made after 2021).
  - It maps jurisdiction and risk in compressed form, recalling Ch 11 without re-describing it.
  - It sorts the proposals on record and tests them against the book's own evidence.
  - It assigns roles by what parties have done.
  - Only its last section argues, labelled once: Udaypur survived by being used, so conserve it as a lived-in town and hill.
- **Distinguishes:**
  - what concludes the historical narrative (the horizon);
  - what emerges from the book as a whole (the protection gap; the voice problem);
  - callbacks by content to §1.1, Ch 7, Ch 10 and Ch 11;
  - what would be redundant (Ch 11's findings; Ch 12's lane).
