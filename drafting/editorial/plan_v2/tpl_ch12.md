# Chapter 12. Udaypur Today: A Living Community and Its Historic Environment

**Target:** 3,750 words · **Sections:** 6 · **Evidence:** `evidence_index/ch12.jsonl` (71 direct, 6 contextual), with the wildlife items moved here from Ch 2 · **Rules:** R5–R8, U7.

## 1. Chapter function

- **Central question:** How do the people of Udaypur live with a thousand-year-old town that others visit as a monument, and whose account of that life does the evidence give?
- **Argument:**
  - Udaypur in the early 2020s was a working village living inside and on top of its medieval town and hill.
  - Visitors and residents rarely met.
  - The record speaks about residents more than for them.
  - The 2021 lane widening is the dated episode where residents' own acts show.

  "Today" means the last record, c. early 2021, stated once.
- **Builds on:** §1.1 (census, houses, the shops, worship, the fair) and §2.3/§2.5 (vegetation, the quarries, Govind Saxena). Recalled in clauses only.
- **Establishes for the Afterword:** residents' hopes and outsiders' plans.

## 2. Arc

A home made from a ruin (12.1) → fields, market and quarries (12.2) → the living hill (12.3) → the temple's visitors (12.4) → the town becoming visible, 2020–21 (12.5) → the lane to the temple, 2021, and whose voice (12.6, into the Afterword).

The scale moves from the household to the village, the hill, Bhopal and back to one lane.

**Opening:** a family's house, found buried in an excavation decades ago, cleaned out and lived in.

**Closing:** residents expecting visitors and trade; outsiders drawing up plans. No recommendation.
