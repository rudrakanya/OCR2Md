## I.2 Evidence rules

Chapter entries cite these by number and do not restate them.

- **R1. Three late records, kept separate.** Three objects are never identified with one another, because no source does so. A draft may compare them, but never merges them.
  - **Record A, "the 1513 record":** Ganguly's fragmentary record "dated 1513 A.D." (*JAOS* VII p. 35). It states that Udayāditya built the temple in V.S. 1116, and Ganguly calls it "very perfunctorily executed". Items: C01-GANGULY-020, C04-GANGULY-093.
  - **Record B, "the Udayapura pedigree":** CII dates it V.S. 1562 (1505/06) and rejects its genealogy (Śūravīra–Gōndala–Gyātā–Udayāditya). It states that Udayāditya was "ruling in Sam 1116, or Śaka 981". Items: C04-CII-131, C04-CII-132, C04-GANGULY-079, C04-GANGULY-081, C04-GANGULY-094, C01-CII-014.
  - **Record C, "the long V.S. 1116 inscription":** twenty-two lines on the temple, read as V.S. 1116 / Śaka 981 by S. K. Verma (reported by Tiwari) and by INTACH, and described as faulty in language and execution. Items: C06-JAGTA-EN-011, C06-INTACH-001, C04-GAZ-006 (the Gazetteer's "two other inscriptions", which are not identified with C).
- **R2. Karṇa is always named by dynasty:** either the **Kalachuri** king of Dāhala (c. 1041–72) or the **Caulukya** king of Gujarat (1064–94). Where an item does not make clear which Karṇa it means, the draft keeps him unspecified and attributes the statement, and the item is listed in the Research Register (item list: Part V, RR-K). Spelling of the dynasty names is pending the user's decision (I.8, D1).
- **R3. The V.S. 1116 statements are not one record.** Record A's V.S. 1116 building date, Record B's "ruling in Sam 1116" and Record C's date stay distinct objects with the labels A, B and C. They are not treated as one record until they have been checked against the primary reports (Bühler, *EI* I; Cunningham, ASR IX–X), which are staged but not yet ingested. The check is Research Register task RR-1.
- **R4. Administrative geography by date.** No administrative, territorial or institutional term is applied before the date of its earliest attesting record.
  - The Bhāillasvāmi-mahādvādaśaka and the Bhṛṅgārikā *pāṭhaka* (sixty-four villages) are attested in Lūṇapasāka's grant of the early 1170s (C01-GANGULY-010; C01-GAZ-010; C04-GAZ-007). They describe twelfth-century arrangements and are never applied to Udayāditya's reign.
  - The "eastern division" governed from Udayapura is attested for 1163 (C08-GANGULY-016).
  - The Mahādvādaśaka-maṇḍala is a twelfth-century name (C04-BHOJDEV-HI-011).
  - The model is Ch 4's treatment of the 1173 record (now §4.6).
- **R5. Testimony.** Tiwari's testimony (JAGTA) is never a measurement, distance, count, date or medieval fact. Verma's readings, as Tiwari reports them, are attributed as such. Tiwari is named in full only in §1.1.
- **R6. One primary home; compatibility is not identity.**
  - An item establishes (E) a claim in one unit only, its home: the unit of its ID prefix unless I.4 names another.
  - Other units cite it as C and tell it in a clause.
  - No two inscriptions, buildings, tanks, traditions, rulers, places, layers or hypotheses are merged unless an item says they are one.
- **R7. Claims stay claims.** A praśasti's statements are what the dynasty claimed. A tradition is evidence for memory. A survey's dates and identifications are proposals. Tier-7 sources are phrased as their own (U8).
- **R8. Proportion.** The Gazetteer supplies at most 40% of a section's cited items. The QC script warns above that.
- **R9. Excluded and staged sources.** *Temple Economics* is excluded (its Udaipur is in Rajasthan). SAMAR items (⚠ old OCR) are normative, are never quoted, and carry the attribution doubt. The staged public-domain sources (Bühler, Beglar, Cunningham, the 1925–26 Gwalior report) are not evidence until hand-corrected and ingested.

## I.8 Decisions still needed

- **D1. Spelling of the dynasty names.** The delivered chapters differ: Ch 1 has Candella / Kalacuri / Caulukya, Ch 3 has Chandella, and Ch 4 has Chandella / Kalachuri / Caulukya. One form is needed for the book. *Default if no ruling:* follow CII's forms, "Chandella", "Kalachuri", "Caulukya" (with "Chālukya" for the Deccan house), and align Ch 1 at the next revision.
