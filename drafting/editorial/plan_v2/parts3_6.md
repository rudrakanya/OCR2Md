## B. The principal argument across Chapter 4 to the Afterword

A king's name and a king's temple are certain; a king's town is not (Ch 4). The building is read for its own making (Ch 5), and the writing for who made it speak (Ch 6). The rest of the fabric is an order of layers more than a calendar (Ch 7). What kept the temple was gifts, not a budget (Ch 8). The town narrates itself (Ch 9). It lost rank in steps under successive masters, among them the Caulukya kings in the twelfth century (U1, Ch 10). It was made known and protected in pieces (Ch 11), and lives on as a working village in an old town, seen mostly by others (Ch 12). The book argues, once and labelled, for conservation by use (AW).

# Part IV. Downstream Drafting Protocol (v2)

1. **Authority:**
   - `chapters.md`;
   - the decisions U1–U9;
   - the rules R1–R9;
   - this outline;
   - `style_profile.md` and `style_sheet.md`.
2. **No paid API calls (U5).** The free checks only: `inhouse.py check`, the prose checks and the ledger check. `ledger.py update` is not run; each chapter's ledger is written by hand and merged with `ledger.py merge`.
3. **Per chapter:**
   1. read the entry and Part I;
   2. print the quotations of every cited item (`_tools/quotes.py`);
   3. write `chNN/chNN_plan_v1.md` (headings `## N.x Title *(scope · NNN w)*`);
   4. make the cfg (model: `ch04/cfg_ch04v1.py`, with BUDGET 0) and the pack (`_pipe/extend_pack.py`);
   5. draft `run/chN/N.x.md` section by section;
   6. verify in-session;
   7. run the checks;
   8. build with `_pipe/build_outputs.py --chapters N`.
4. **Flexible structure.** The section count and lengths are set per chapter in Part II, between 4 and 8 sections, each roughly 400–1,500 words. A drafting session does not add, remove, merge or reorder sections; that is a structural change for the user. A section may land at 70–120% of its target. Never pad. If evidence runs out, report the shortfall with its reason.
5. **In-session verification:**
   - every factual sentence is checked against its cited items' quotations;
   - every quotation is matched verbatim (the validator checks it);
   - pages come from the item locators;
   - anything unverifiable goes in `chNN_flags.md`.

   Every deliverable says "verified in-session, not by an independent model".
6. **Rules cited, not restated.** R1–R9 and U1–U9 govern every chapter. Specifically:
   - Karṇa always takes his dynasty (R2);
   - administrative terms are dated (R4);
   - Records A, B and C are kept separate (R1, R3);
   - the Gazetteer's claims are phrased as its own (U8);
   - no testimony supplies a measure (R5).
7. **Evidence roles:**
   - E items establish, and only in their home unit (R6);
   - C items corroborate or cross-refer;
   - X items give context;
   - Note items feed `{{note: …}}`.

   Items marked optional are omitted unless a section would land below 70%.
8. **Gap-filling:** free local search only, through `_tools/kb_new.py`: verbatim text, page from the marker, tier, `kb-new`.
9. **What needs the user:** any structural change, any conflict the outline keeps open, and the legal-review passages (LR-1, LR-2).
