## B. The principal argument, Chapter 1 to the Afterword

1. **Ch 1.** An ordinary village lives inside a king's town, and everything it keeps reaches back to one eleventh-century act of naming, which may have been a founding or a taking-over.
2. **Ch 2.** The land made that town possible without deciding it: water had to be made, and the town was cut from its own hill.
3. **Ch 3.** The country was old, named and carved long before, but its past reaches the hill only as proposals, and by the tenth century the range was a crowded frontier.
4. **Ch 4.** A king's name and a king's temple are certain; a king's town is not.
5. **Ch 5.** The building is read for its own making.
6. **Ch 6.** The writing is read for who made it speak.
7. **Ch 7.** The rest of the fabric is an order of layers more than a calendar.
8. **Ch 8.** What kept the temple was gifts, not a budget.
9. **Ch 9.** The town narrates itself.
10. **Ch 10.** It lost rank in steps under successive masters, the Caulukya kings among them in the twelfth century (U1).
11. **Ch 11.** It was made known and protected in pieces.
12. **Ch 12.** It lives on as a working village in an old town, seen mostly by others.
13. **Afterword.** The book argues, once and labelled, for conservation by use.

# Part IV. Downstream Drafting Protocol

*(Applies when the pause is lifted.)*

1. **Authority:**
   - `chapters.md`;
   - the decisions U1–U13;
   - the rules R1–R11;
   - this outline;
   - `style/style_profile.md` and `style/style_sheet.md`.
2. **No paid API calls (U5).**
   - Use only the free checks: `inhouse.py check`, which includes the prose checks, the dynasty-spelling check and the ledger check.
   - `ledger.py update` is not run. Each chapter's ledger is written by hand and merged with `ledger.py merge`.
   - The Chroma vector collection is not queried, since every query costs an embedding call.
3. **Per chapter:**
   1. read the entry and Part I;
   2. print the quotations of every cited item (`_tools/quotes.py`);
   3. write `chNN/chNN_plan_v1.md`, with headings `## N.x Title *(scope · NNN w)*`;
   4. make the cfg (model `ch04/cfg_ch04v1.py`, BUDGET 0) and the pack (`_pipe/extend_pack.py`);
   5. draft `run/chN/N.x.md` section by section;
   6. verify in-session;
   7. run the checks;
   8. stitch (`_tools/stitch.py N`) and build (`_pipe/build_outputs.py --chapters N`).
4. **Structure.** Each chapter keeps the number and shape of sections its entry sets. A drafting session does not add, remove, merge or reorder sections; that is a structural change for the user.
   - A section may land at 70–120% of its target.
   - Never pad. If the evidence runs out, report the shortfall with its reason.
5. **Chapters already written (1–4).** Changes to them are light-touch fixes listed in RR-42, or decided revisions (U12, RR-40). After any change: run `_tools/reconcile.py`, which re-derives the as-written entry, then `_tools/delivered_entries.py` and the assembler.
6. **In-session verification.**
   - Every factual sentence is checked against its cited items' quotations.
   - Every quotation is matched verbatim (the validator checks this).
   - Pages come from the item locators.
   - Anything unverifiable is logged in `chNN_flags.md`.
   - Every deliverable says "verified in-session, not by an independent model".
7. **Rules cited, not restated.**
   - R1–R11 and U1–U13 govern every chapter.
   - Karṇa always takes his dynasty (R2), in IAST (U10).
   - Administrative terms are dated (R4).
   - Records A, B and C stay separate (R1, R3).
   - The Gazetteer's claims are its own (U8).
   - No testimony supplies a measure (R5).
   - The settled ranges are used as ranges (R10).
8. **Evidence roles.**
   - E items establish, and only in their home unit (R6).
   - C items corroborate or cross-refer; X items give context; Note items feed `{{note: …}}`.
   - Items marked optional are omitted unless a section would land below 70%.
9. **Gap-filling.** Free local text or BM25 search only, through `_tools/kb_new.py`: verbatim text, page from the marker, tier, `kb-new`. If local search cannot answer, add a research flag; never spend money.
10. **What needs the user:**
    - any structural change;
    - any conflict this outline keeps open;
    - the legal-review passages (LR-1, LR-2);
    - D5–D7.
