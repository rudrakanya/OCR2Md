## 12.1 Population and the census record *(1901 to 2011; the only firm counts)*

1. **P1 — The count of 2011.** Below the hill, about 1,247 houses and 6,383 people; men and women counted apart. The 2011 figures were given in 1.1; recall and add the division. "Today" defined once. E: C12-GEOHER-001, C12-INTACH-002, C01-GEOHER-003.
2. **P2 — 1901 and 1971.** Under two thousand in 1901 (928 or 1,619, both printed, RR-26); 2,025 in 1971. The village grew more slowly than the district's rural population; it trebled after 1971. E: C12-GAZ-008, C12-GAZ-004.
3. **P3 — The district behind the village; what the census is not.** The parganas of 1901 and the district's near trebling; 1921 and 1931 as a lesson in the census's own limits; a neighbouring hamlet for scale; Tiwari's round "eight thousand" is not a count (note). E: C10-GAZ-052, C10-GAZ-053, C12-INTACH-003, C12-JAGTA-HI-001.
