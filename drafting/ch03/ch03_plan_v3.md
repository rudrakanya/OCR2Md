**Historical question.** What stood in this country before Udaypur, and how much of it reached as far as the town's hill?

**Through-line.** By the end, the reader knows the country's old names and its few prehistoric traces, Vidisha as literature remembers it and as excavation has found it, the dynasties that held it from the Mauryas to the Guptas, the roads and donors that tied it to a wider world, its early shrines and images, the Gupta caves and flat-roofed temples that brought worship in stone close to Udaypur, the move from Vidisha to Bhilsa and the Sun image that named it, and the contested frontier of the eighth to tenth centuries on Udaypur's own range, ending on the open question of who held the hill.
