**Through-line.** By the end, the reader knows the land the founder found: a river country whose river does not reach the town, a sandstone hill worn from a tableland, one wet season with failures in runs, black soil that carries a winter crop on held water, thin forest long grazed and cut, water that had to be made, and a seam of stone that built the temple and still builds the village.
