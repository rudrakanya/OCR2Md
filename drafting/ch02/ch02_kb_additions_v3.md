On the destruction of Besnagar, located on the western side of the Betwa sometime after 7th century A.D., a new town sprang up on the eastern bank of the river. This new town was known as Bhailaswamin or Bhillaswamin, so named after the god sun, to whom a temple was dedicated here. The name of the place was later corrupted to Bhilsa, when Al Beruni visited the town in 11th century A.D.
