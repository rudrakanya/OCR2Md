**Historical question.** How did an ordinary village in Basoda tahsil come to hold the remains, records and memories of a far more important place, and what kind of royal act stands at its beginning?

**Through-line.** By the end, the reader knows the village as it is, the king whose name it carries and the kingdom he ruled, the dates and records that tie him to it, the masters who held it after him, how the place has been known and remembered, and why the question of whether he founded it or took it over stays open.

**Arc.** The village and its temple as they are (1.1–1.3) → the name and the king (1.4–1.6) → the date and the seat of government (1.7–1.8) → later masters (1.9) → how the place is known: the praśasti, the inscriptions, the legends (1.10–1.12) → how it has been ranked (1.13) → the open question, ending on the ground both answers stand on (1.14, into Chapter 2).
