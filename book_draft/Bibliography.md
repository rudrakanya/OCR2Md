# Bibliography

*Consolidated from the sources cited across all chapters. Works marked "(cited via …)" were quoted as they appear within another source consulted for this book, not consulted directly.*

## Primary texts and translations
- **Samarāṅgaṇasūtradhāra** of Mahārājādhirāja Śrī Bhojadeva (vāstuśāstra). Chapters 49–50 and others consulted in translation; selected chapters also reproduced in the appendix to Pande, *The Udayeśvara Temple*.
- **Udayapur Praśasti** — the foundation eulogy of the Udayeśvara temple (quoted in Pande, *The Udayeśvara Temple*, and in the Paramāra epigraphic corpora).
- Abū'l-Faẓl. *Āʾīn-i Akbarī*. (Cited via Ganguly, *History of the Paramāra Dynasty*.)

## Inscriptions and epigraphic corpora
- Trivedi, Harihar Vitthal, ed. *Inscriptions of the Paramāras, Chandēllas, Kachchapaghātas and Two Minor Dynasties*. Corpus Inscriptionum Indicarum, Vol. VII, Pts. 2–3. Archaeological Survey of India (Trivedi 1978, 1989).
- Mittal, A. C. *The Inscriptions of Imperial Paramāras (800 A.D. to 1320 A.D.)*. Ahmedabad: L. D. Institute of Indology, 1979.
- Sastri, K. N. "Three Paramāra Inscriptions from Malwa." *Epigraphia Indica*, Vol. XXXI (1955–56), ed. D. C. Sircar. Delhi. (Cited via S. Singh.)

## Modern scholarship
- Pande, Anupa. *The Udayeśvara Temple: Art, Architecture and Philosophy of the Śaiva Siddhānta*. Archaeological Survey of India, Bhopal Circle. — *the principal source for this book.*
- Patil, D. R. *The Cultural Heritage of Madhya Bharat*. Gwalior: Department of Archaeology, Government of Madhya Bharat, 1952.
- Ganguly, D. C. *History of the Paramāra Dynasty*. Dacca, 1933.
- Singh, Mahesh. *Bhoja Paramāra and His Times*.
- Singh, Saarthak. "A Serpentine Scimitar of Letters from Udaypur, District Vidisha, M.P."
- Venziya, Pratapsinh R. "History of the Parmar Rajput Dynasty." *The Academic: International Journal of Multidisciplinary Research* 3, no. 11 (2025).
- Krishna Deva. *Temples of North India*. New Delhi: National Book Trust, 1969.
- Adhikari, Swati Mondal. *Some Paramāra Temples from Madhya Pradesh: A Case Study of Village Un*. Bhopal: Directorate of Archaeology, Archives and Museums.
- Kramrisch, Stella. *The Hindu Temple*. Calcutta, 1946. (Cited via Adhikari, *Some Paramāra Temples from Madhya Pradesh*.)
- Salomon, Richard. *Indian Epigraphy: A Guide to the Study of Inscriptions in Sanskrit, Prakrit, and the Other Indo-Aryan Languages*. Oxford, 1998; and review in *Journal of the American Oriental Society* 102, no. 3 (1982). (Cited via S. Singh.)
- Pollock, Sheldon. *The Language of the Gods in the World of Men: Sanskrit, Culture, and Power in Premodern India*. Berkeley, 2006. (Cited via S. Singh.)
- Kumar, Amit, et al. "Streamflow of the Betwa River under the Combined Effect of LU-LC and Climate Change." Indian Institute of Science Education and Research, Bhopal.
