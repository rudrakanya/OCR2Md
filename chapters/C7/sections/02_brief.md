It may be assumed that the Bhūmija temples had a pañacratha plan before the development of their saptaratha drama. In the extant temples of Mālavā, however, both the pañacratha and saptaratha plans occur simultaneously. The pañacratha temples show on the śikkara a vertical row of three śikkatambhas in each quadrant. One such example is the Mahākāḷeśvara temple No.2 at Un. In the saptaratha temples, there are five vertical rows of śikkatambhas in each quadrant. The Nīlakantheśvara temple at Un belongs to this category. The śikkatambhas in horizontal row could rise either in pañacrathāma (five storeyed) or saptabhūma (seven storeyed) stages.
