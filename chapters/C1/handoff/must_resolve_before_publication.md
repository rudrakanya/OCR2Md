## 2. The 1070 accession framing — confirm the wording stands

**Where:** §1.5, and referenced in §1.4.

The chapter adopts your instructed wording: **Udayāditya ruled Mālava from about 1070 until the early 1090s, and was already active at Udaypur by 1059.** The Stance section states which half of that is secure and which is a choice:

| span | source | chunk |
|---|---|---|
| 1070–1093 | INTACH 2022, numismatic section | `intach-2022-udaypur-00075` |
| c. 1059–93 | INTACH 2022, **same volume, different page** | `intach-2022-udaypur-00127` |
| 1058–1087 | Patil 1952 | `patil-1952-00095` |
| 1059–1086 | Ganguly | `ganguly-paramara-00568` |

The epigraphic difficulty is stated in the chapter: the Panhera inscription of V.S. 1116 (1059 CE) was issued by a feudatory of Jayasiṃha, which places the paramount throne with Jayasiṃha in the year a Udaypur record shows Udayāditya ruling (`ganguly-paramara-00262`, `ganguly-paramara-00284`). Ganguly's reconciliation — that Udayāditya held his paternal territory without yet being sovereign — is labelled `speculation` in the knowledge base and is attributed as a surmise in the prose (`ganguly-paramara-00274`).

**How §1.5 now handles it.** The accession is argued from both sides rather than asserted, and each case carries its own evidence:

| | evidence in the chapter | chunks |
|---|---|---|
| **early, 1058–59** | Udayāditya founding temple and tank in 1059 on a royal scale; Patil's 1058–1087 with no interval named between the king's death and the revival; Ganguly's own "Paramara Udayaditya (1059-1086 A. D.)"; INTACH's c. 1059–93; the succession crisis after Jayasiṃha's death, with no other prince strong enough | `patil-composite-inscriptions-00084`, `intach-geoheritage-00092`, `patil-1952-00095`, `ganguly-paramara-00568`, `intach-2022-udaypur-00127`, `ganguly-paramara-00284` |
| **c. 1070** | the Panhera inscription of the same year, issued by a feudatory of Jayasiṃha; Mālava obtained by defeating Karṇa, who was not yet king in 1059; INTACH's numismatic 1070–1093 | `ganguly-paramara-00262`, `ganguly-paramara-00279`, `ganguly-paramara-00274`, `intach-2022-udaypur-00075` |
| **agreed by both** | building at Udaypur in 1059; ruling at Dhārā by 1077 and still in 1086 | `patil-composite-inscriptions-00084`, `ganguly-paramara-00293`, `ganguly-paramara-00290` |

**Decision required:** confirm the wording stands, or choose an earlier accession — in which case §1.4's final paragraph and §1.5's third anchor both need revising together.
