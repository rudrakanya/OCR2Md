Today, the 26th of October 2025 Sunday, I am rediscovering one of my latent arts - of writing creative non-fiction. It is such a joy & ecstasy for me, to revel in my own voice & echo my inner musings through the written word. It is triggering the recollection of a phrase I heard from my guru, "the Book of Hearts". I now reclaim & re-declare my iron intent - to begin in earnest the solemn task of expressing everything in me which can be verbalized - and finding words for those expressions which can be alluded to. It occurs to me that the dying spark within me can be restored & revived by this writing - revitalizing the intellectual instinct that is slaked in thirst of externalization in a world (socially) that is ill equipped to contain or even dip into the oceans of my depths.

I watched a Youtube video today, "I am politely begging you to write nonfiction", where I learned about 3 forms of writing - fiction, poetry & non-fiction. Fiction may be of fantasy or more reality grounded figments of the writer's imagination. Poetry & its many forms - some which adhere strictly to rigid formats of rhyme, rhythm, syllable - and others which cast off all the rules with a spirit of adventurous abandon. But the primary premise of the video was to introduce the viewer to a non-academic style of non fiction writing - creative non fiction. At first I struggled to grasp what the video was trying to convey, the tag "non-fiction" being tightly wound by years of association with textbooks, reference material & so on in the shadowy & ill frequented corners of school library racks. Their stifling silence echoed loudly within me - where the fiction racks offered the only refuge into a world worth journeying into.
