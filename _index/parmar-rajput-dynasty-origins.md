# Index: parmar-rajput-dynasty-origins.md

**Title/Author:** "History of the Parmar Rajput Dynasty" by Dr. Pratapsinh Ranaji Venziya (Assistant Professor of History, Shri Anand Arts College, Eta, Gujarat). Published in *The Academic: International Journal of Multidisciplinary Research*, Vol. 3, Issue 11, Nov 2025 (ISSN 2583-973X; DOI 10.5281/zenodo.17896750).

**Overall (3-5 sentences):** A research paper tracing the origin, rise, and fall of the Paramara (Parmar) Rajput dynasty using inscriptions, copperplates, and literary/bardic sources. It argues from epigraphic and literary evidence (Padmagupta's *Navasahasankacharitra*, Abul Fazl's *Ain-i-Akbari*, the *Udaipur Praśasti*) that the Parmars are the true Agnikula (fire-born) lineage originating from sage Vashishtha's *yajna-kunda* on Mount Abu. It chronicles the Malwa-based rulers from Upendra (c. AD 800) through the dynasty's zenith under Vakpati Munj, Sindhuraj, and Bhoja, to its decline by the 13th-14th centuries. The dynasty's seat was Malwa with capitals at Ujjain and Dhara (Dharnagari).

**Authoritative on:** Paramara/Parmar dynastic genealogy and chronology; the Agnikula origin myth; King Bhoja's reign, inscriptions, and scholarship; primary sources for Paramara history.

**Relevance to a monograph on the Udayeśvara temple of Udaypur:** Supporting/light. The paper is about the dynasty broadly, not the temple. However, the Udayeśvara temple at Udaypur (Madhya Pradesh) was built by the later Paramara king Udayaditya, so this gives essential dynastic and chronological background. Note: it does NOT cover Udayaditya or the temple itself; the recurring "Udaipur Praśasti" / "Udaipur reference" cited here refers to the Paramara inscription (in *Vir Vinod*, ed. Shyamaldas), a key epigraphic source, which may itself be relevant.

## Section map

### Abstract
- States the Parmars belong to the Agnikula lineage, born from Vashishtha's sacrificial fire; cites Dr. Dasharatha Sharma: *"No dynasty other than the Paramaras should be regarded as an Agnikula lineage."*
- Lists supporting archaeological sites: Vasantgarh, Udaipur, Nagpur, Harthal, Delwara, Patnarayan, Achaleshwar.

### Introduction
- Origin from the fire pit (Agni Kund) of Rishi Vashishtha on Mount Abu (Arbudachala); four clans produced — Chauhans, Pariharas, Parmars, Solankis (Chaulukyas). Also tied to *Ramayana* Balkand ch. 54-55 (Vishwamitra/Kamadhenu).
- Sources: Padmagupta's *Navasahasankacharitra* (composed under Sindhuraja), Abul Fazl's *Ain-i-Akbari* (sage "Mahabahu" = Vashishtha; ~2,350 yrs before Elahi Samvat), *Prithviraj Raso*.

### Rulers of the Early Parmar Dynasty
- Primary seat Malwa; titles *Malvapati* and *Avanti Naresh*; capitals Ujjain and Dharnagari. Empire extent: Kota/Bundi (N), Bhilsa/Hoshangabad/Sagar (E), Godavari/Khandesh (S), Mahi river (W).
- Sub-rulers: **Upendra** (AD 800-818, first king); **Vairi Singh I** (836-843); **Siyaka I** (843-893); **Vakpati I** (893-918); **Vairi Singh II / Vajrat** (918-948).

### Great Kings: Vakpati II, Sindhuraj, and Bhoj
- **Vakpati Munj** (AD 973-995): adopted son of Siyak II; defeated Tailap II ~16 times but captured/killed on 17th; titles Srivallabh, Prithvi Vallabh, Amodhavarsha; built Munj Sagar lake at Dhar; patron of Padmagupta, Dhananjaya, Halayudha.
- **Sindhuraj** (late 10th c.): brother of Munj, titles Narayan/Sahasank, father of Bhoja. Subsection covers the War with the Huns (Siyaka II's NW campaigns; conflict with Chandelas at Khajuraho).

### Great King Bhoj (AD 1000-1055)
- Ninth major ruler; birth-prediction legend (55 yr reign) and Munj's attempt to dispose of him; polymath who wrote ~84 books; proverb *"Kahan Raja Bhoj, Kahan Gangu Taili."* Couplet *"अद्य धारा सदाधारा..."* (in life) vs *"अद्य धारा निराधारा..."* (on death).
- Subsections: Key epigraphic evidences (Banswara 1020, Betma 1020, Ujjain 1021, Saraswati Pratima/British Museum, Tilakwada copperplate Samvat 1103, Kallavan/Nashik); Military confrontations (Indraratha, Lat region, aid to Anandapala vs Mahmud of Ghazni).

### Fall of the Parmar Dynasty
- Late-12th-c. decline; exhaustive wars with Gujarat Chaulukyas (Arjunavarman, Jayasimha); Yadavas of Devagiri invaded Malwa; Delhi Sultanate breached borders; Visaladeva of Gujarat (acc. AD 1243) invaded per Dabhoi inscription.
- Dynasty ruled 8th c. to first half of 14th c.; collapsed from multi-front wars and economic depletion.

## Key entities, dates & terms
- **Agnikula / Agnivamsa** — fire-born lineage origin myth (Mount Abu, Vashishtha's yajna-kunda).
- **Upendra** (800-818) — first Paramara king. **Vakpati Munj** (973-995). **Sindhuraj** — father of Bhoja. **Bhoja** (1000-1055) — greatest king, 9th ruler.
- **Siyaka II** — sovereign (Chakravarti) ruler; fought Hunas and Chandelas.
- **Capitals:** Ujjain, Dhara/Dharnagari (Malwa).
- **Padmagupta (Parimala)** — court poet, *Navasahasankacharitra*. **Abul Fazl** — *Ain-i-Akbari*/*Akbarnama*.
- **Udaipur Praśasti** — Paramara inscription (in *Vir Vinod*, ed. Shyamaldas, Mewar State Press) — key genealogical source.
- **Inscriptions:** Banswara (1020), Betma (1020), Ujjain (1021), Tilakwada copperplate (Samvat 1103), Kallavan (Nashik), Dabhoi.
- **Key sources cited:** Ganguly 2013, Jain 1972, Shastri 1867, Dwivedi 1986 (*Prabandhachintamani*), Mathur 1969.
