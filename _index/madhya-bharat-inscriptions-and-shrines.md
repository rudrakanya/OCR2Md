# Index: madhya-bharat-inscriptions-and-shrines.md

**Title/Author:** "Archeological & Cultural Heritage of Madhya Bharat: Shrines, Architecture, and Inscriptions." A composite file: Part I by Dr. D. R. Patil (Dept. of Archaeology, Madhya Bharat, Gwalior, 1952); the Un shrines analysis by Swati Mondal Adhikari (Directorate of Archaeology, Bhopal); Part III by Harihar Vitthal Trivedi, *Corpus Inscriptionum Indicarum* Vol. VII Pt. 2 (Inscriptions of the Paramāras of Malwa, ASI).

**Overall (3-5 sentences):** A four-part regional handbook covering the cultural geography, history, art and architecture of Madhya Bharat, followed by a site-by-site directory of premier monuments, a technical architectural study of the ten Bhūmija temples at village Un, an edited corpus of early Paramāra copper-plate inscriptions (with Sanskrit texts), and a Sanskrit-English architectural glossary. The historical chapters trace the region from Puranic times through the Pradyotas, Mauryas, Guptas, Pratiharas/Rashtrakutas, the Paramāras and Kacchavahas, and the Malwa Sultanate to the Marathas. Strong emphasis on Paramāra temple architecture (Bhūmija/Nagara style) and on the dynasty's earliest epigraphy. The file's unique value lies in Parts II-IV, which the header note flags as not shared with the two companion Madhya Bharat files.

**Authoritative on:** Paramāra dynastic history and inscriptions (Harsōlā, Ahmedabad, Dharampuri, Ujjain, Gaonri grants of Sīyaka and Vākpati Munja); the Bhūmija temple style and its technical vocabulary; the Un temple cluster (architecture, masons' marks, chronology); monument inventory of Madhya Bharat districts.

**Relevance to a monograph on the Udayeśvara temple of Udaypur: CORE.** Contains a dedicated Udaypur site entry describing the Udayeśvara (Nilkantheśvara) temple in detail, names King Udayāditya as founder with precise dates (commenced 1059 A.D. / V.S. 1116; flagstaff hoisted 1080 A.D. / V.S. 1137), discusses its seven-storeyed Bhūmija śikhara, and references its founding praśasti inscriptions on the porches. It also supplies the full Paramāra dynastic and epigraphic context (Siyaka II onward), the Bhūmija style's canonical mechanics, and the Un cluster's chronology which dates a phase to "Reign of King Udayaditya (c. 1075-1100 A.D.)." Note: the Un Nīlakantheśvara temple is a separate Un shrine, not the Udaypur temple — do not conflate. The Naravarman / Udayapur Praśasti are not explicitly named here, but Udayāditya's praśasti-bearing porch inscriptions are.

## Section map

### PART I, Ch. I-III (Formation, Geography, Peoples)
- Formation of Madhya Bharat (22 states, covenant 22 Apr 1948); five natural divisions keyed to Chambal/Betwa/Narmada and Vindhya/Satpura systems; population, languages (Western Hindi, Malwi, Nimadi).

### Ch. IV: Historical Pre-View
- Puranic Haihayas/Mahishmati through Pradyotas, Mauryas (Ashoka at Ujjain), Shungas (Heliodoros pillar), Guptas (Udaygiri), Hunas, Yashodharman.
- Paramāras of Malwa: Siyaka II, Vakpati Munja, Bhoja the Great (1010-1050), **Udayaditya (1058-1087) who built the Nilakanteshvar temple at Udaypur**; Kacchavahas of Gwalior (Sas Bahu 1093); Sultanate, Tomars, Marathas.

### Ch. V: Arts and Architecture
- Sacred/Muslim/civil architecture; cites the **Udayeshwar Temple at Udaypur (built by Udayaditya Paramara 1059-1080)** among medieval golden-age temples alongside Nemawar, Sas Bahu, Un. Sculpture, painting (Bagh Caves), Gwalior music school, crafts.

### Ch. VI: Archaeological Monuments
- 1,783 monuments / 467 places; 145 of national importance. District counts; chronological sequence; typology (~240 Shaiva temples incl. Udayeshwar; forts, sati stones, tombs, stepwells, sarais).

### Part II: Directory of Premier Sites
- Northern MB (Gwalior, Pawaya, Suhania, Padhavli-Mitaoli, Narwar, Kadwaha, Ranod, Terahi, Mahua, Chanderi).
- Bhilsa district incl. **Udaypur entry** — Udayeśvara/Nilkantheśvara temple full description; also Bijamandal, Bara-khambi, Shahi Masjid; Tughlaq mosque at rear (1336-1338 A.D. / A.H. 737-739).
- Malwa (Ujjain, Dhar/Bhoja Shala, Mandu), Mandsaur/Sondni, Nimar (Bagh, Maheshwar, Nemawar Siddheshvar Bhumija temple).

### Un Shrines: Technical Analysis (Swati Mondal Adhikari)
- Ten Bhūmija temples under Paramāra patronage; core mechanics of the style (latā, kūṭastambhas, śukanāsā, talacchanda, vedībandha, jaṅghā).
- Per-temple directory (Chaubara Dera 1-2, Mahākāleśvara 1-2, Nameless, Haṭakeśvara, Nīlakantheśvara, Omkāreśvara, Ballāleśvara, Goaleśvara). Chaubara Dera No. 1 holds the **Sarpabandha Inscription** and a separate inscription naming **King Bhoja**. Goaleśvara dated by inscription to 1206 A.D. (V.S. 1263), donated by Jain Acharya Yashakirti.
- Masons' marks & Chirakhan quarry; chronological stratigraphy table (c.1000-1206 A.D.), Phase 5 = "Reign of King Udayaditya, c. 1075-1100."

### Part III: Corpus Inscriptionum Indicarum (Trivedi) — Paramāra grants
- Methodological addenda; then edited inscriptions Nos. 1-7 with Sanskrit text (esp. Harsōlā Grant A).
- No. 1-2 Harsōlā plates of Sīyaka (V.S. 1005 / 949 A.C.) — earliest Paramāra records.
- No. 3 Ahmedabad grant of Sīyaka (V.S. 1026 / 969). No. 4 Dharampuri grant of Vākpati Munja (V.S. 1031 / 974) — earliest Malwa charter. No. 5 Ujjain grant (V.S. 1036). Nos. 6-7 Gaonri plates (V.S. 1038, 1043) — incl. palimpsest over Rashtrakuta record; 36 Brahmana donees.

### Part IV: Sanskrit-English Architectural Glossary
- ~45 terms with definitions and cite-tags (Āmalaka, Bhadra, Bhūmija, Garbhagṛha, Jaṅghā, Kūṭastambha, Latā, Śikhara terms, Vedībandha, Vṛtta, etc.).

## Key entities, dates & terms
- **Udayāditya (Paramāra):** r. 1058-1087; founder of the Udayeśvara/Nilkantheśvara temple at Udaypur; restored Paramāra fortunes.
- **Udayeśvara (Nilkantheśvara) temple, Udaypur:** Bhūmija masterpiece, red sandstone; founded 1059 A.D. (V.S. 1116), flag hoisted 1080 A.D. (V.S. 1137); seven-storeyed śikhara; founding Sanskrit inscriptions on porches; brass linga face plate gifted 1775 by Khande Rao Appaji; rear Tughlaq mosque 1336-1338.
- **Bhoja the Great:** r. 1010-1050; Bhoja Shala at Dhar; named in Chaubara Dera No.1 inscription at Un.
- **Sīyaka II (Siyaka):** Paramāra, Rashtrakuta feudatory turned sovereign; Harsōlā/Ahmedabad grants (949, 969 A.C.).
- **Vākpati II Munja:** r. 974-995; Dharampuri, Ujjain, Gaonri grants.
- **Bhūmija style:** Nagara sub-style; latā spines + kūṭastambha quadrants; key term throughout Un, Udaypur, Nemawar, Gyaraspur Maladevi.
- **Naga/Gupta/Pratihara-Rashtrakuta context:** Padmavati (Pawaya), Udaygiri caves, Heliodoros pillar (Besnagar), Pathari Garudadhvaja (861 A.D.).
- **Kacchavahas of Gwalior:** Sas Bahu (1093), Suhania Kakanmadh (~1000).
- **Sarpabandha Inscription:** serpentine grammatical chart, Un Chaubara Dera No.1 (parallels at Ujjain Mahākāla & Dhar).
- **V.S. (Vikrama Samvat) dating** used throughout (V.S. + ~57 = A.D.).
